About my father

today I want to talk about my dad
He is my hero

My father has always been my biggest supporter. He has always stood by my side, encouraged me, and sincerely wants me to succeed and make progress in life. Unfortunately, he believes that doctors are more successful than people in other professions, but I have a different dream—I want to become a professional programmer. I want to prove to my father that engineers and programmers can also be successful, influential, and important people. My greatest wish is to make him proud through my achievements and to see him happy because of my success.
