Improves vocabulary

Books help us increase our knowledge. We can learn about history, science, technology, and many other subjects. Reading also improves vocabulary and writing skills, especially for people who are learning English.

Another benefit of reading is that it helps us relax. After a busy day, reading a good story can reduce stress and improve our mood. Many people read before going to bed because it helps them sleep better.
